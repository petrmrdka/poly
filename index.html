— 01 / The Practice

Three disciplines.
One programme.

01 / Strength

Power
& Force

— Foundational lifts

Compound strength built on push, pull, squat and hinge. Programmed in cycles that respect the competitive calendar — heavy off-season, refined in-season — with functional patterns that transfer directly to ice, fairway and court.

02 / Speed

Acceleration
& Agility

— Velocity training

First-step quickness, change-of-direction mechanics, plyometric power, reactive sprint work. The sport-specific velocity layer that turns prepared muscle into competitive separation when the puck drops or the ball lands.

03 / Resilience

Injury
Prevention

— Longevity protocols

Joint integrity, controlled mobility, tissue capacity, recovery architecture. The unglamorous work behind a long career — the discipline that lets a professional athlete still be performing in their thirties.
